# Why this is not settled here

Because it cannot, and because the reason it cannot is instructive. Both readings are compatible with the investigating authority's findings, which are about training and monitoring, and *training* is exactly where a cultural explanation and an institutional explanation converge. An operator whose training does not produce cross-checking will produce accidents whether the underlying reason is a national authority gradient or a badly designed syllabus. Distinguishing those requires evidence about crews who did not crash, and that evidence is the subject of the next section, because it mostly does not exist.
