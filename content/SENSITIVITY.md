# Sensitivity markers

Keys final-moments passages and unsettled matters to a reader-facing control whose default is collapsed.

## Levels

**standard** — Historical case, concluded investigation, no live proceedings. Renders normally.

**elevated** — Contains material describing the final minutes, or concerns a matter where surviving relatives are organised and publicly active. Final-moments passages sit behind a control that is collapsed by default and labelled so the reader knows what expanding it will show. The reader may set the control to remain collapsed throughout, and the piece must remain complete and lose no argument if they do.

**quarantined** — Unsettled matter, active proceedings, or an investigation not concluded at the research cutoff. Rendered only as procedural fact — what body is investigating, what stage it has reached, what the law permits to be disclosed. No findings, no characterisation of crew conduct, no description of recording content.

## The rule the levels serve

The charter forbids hosting, linking, embedding or describing cockpit audio. A reported description of recording content — a prosecutor's characterisation, a leaked summary, a preliminary report's paraphrase — sits at the edge of that rule. The ruling applied here: **reported descriptions of recording content are inside the prohibition.** They are not reproduced, summarised or characterised. Where the existence of such a description is itself the subject — as in the section on leaks — the fact that a leak occurred is stated and its content is not.
