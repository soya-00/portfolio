Overriding constraints

These sit above every section and resolve any conflict within them.

The central question is open. What did investigators actually hear, and how did different countries decide what it meant? The piece does not assume CVR evidence caused CRM. The causal weight of recorded evidence against regulatory pressure, insurance pressure, manufacturer influence, union politics, and institutional fashion is a live question the piece keeps live. A draft that resolves into "the recordings revealed the problem and CRM was the answer" has failed this charter regardless of how well it reads.

Equal weight. History and transcript material receive comparable space, depth, and design attention. Neither is illustration for the other.

International throughout. National and regional difference is the spine of the piece, not a comparative sidebar late in the running order. Any section that could have been written about the United States alone needs rewriting.

Breadth first. The core path covers the whole territory at survey depth. Depth arrives through optional layers and onward references, never by deepening the core path at the cost of coverage.

Nothing on screen that is not in the corpus. This is the one rule with no exceptions and no editorial discretion.
